RESPONSIBLE PARTIES: Medical Management Administration, Pharmacy Department, Utilization Management, Integrated Care Management, Claims Department 3. DEFINITIONS: Keytruda is a monoclonal antibody that acts as an immune checkpoint inhibitor by binding to the human programmed death-1 (PD-1) receptor on T cells and blocking the PD-1 interaction with PD-1 ligands (L1, L2) which allows activated tumor-specific T cells to kill tumor cells and secrete cytokines to restore antitumor immune responses.
